25 August · with you

A letter

For my Sir

Dear Prem Sir,

Every day I sit in front of you. It looks simple from outside — a student, a teacher, a table between us. For me it is the safest place in the room. You call me Guruji, and that one word carries so much affection that I forget how heavy the day was before I arrived.

You teach without making it feel like a lesson. You correct without making me small. You laugh, and the whole front row becomes lighter. I do not always have the right words when we are together. So I made this page, because birthdays deserve the words we keep in our chest.

Yesterday was short. I still keep thinking about it. Some meetings are long and empty. Ours was small and full. Thank you for that picture. Thank you for letting me sit in front of you. Thank you for calling me Guruji like it is the most natural thing in the world.

May this year be kind to you. May your work stay meaningful. May your health stay strong. May people sit in front of you the way I do — quietly grateful, secretly proud, always a little happier because you are there.

Happy birthday, Sir.
With respect, with a smile, with a full heart —

Your Guruji

Three small wishes

Happy. Emotional. True.

The front seat

I always sit in front. Not to be seen — to listen. From that place I have learned more than skills. I have learned how a good human holds a room.

That name, Guruji

When you say Guruji, it feels like a blessing and a joke and a hug at the same time. Please never stop saying it. It is my favourite sound in class.

A year of light

May your days stay bright like yesterday’s smile. May worries stay small. May you keep collecting little moments like this photograph — simple, warm, and ours.

Blow the candle, Sir.
I already made the wish for you.

Happy Birthday, Prem Sir

From the student who always sits in front — Guruji
